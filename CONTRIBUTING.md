# Contributing to Kairos-Bridge AI Journal

Thanks for your interest in improving the journal.

## Contributor License Agreement (required)

This project is **dual-licensed**: free to the public under the GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later, and separately under paid commercial licenses (see [`COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md)). To keep that possible, the copyright holder needs permission to include every contribution in both kinds of license.

So **before an outside contribution (code, documentation, tests, anything) can be merged, its author must sign the Contributor License Agreement** in [`CLA.md`](CLA.md). With it, you keep the copyright in your contribution and grant Steffan Douglas Haskins a broad license to use it, including the right to sublicense it under the AGPL and under commercial terms.

> The CLA is currently a **DRAFT pending attorney review**. Until the final version is published, contributions can't be accepted.

How to sign (once the final CLA is published): fill in the form at the end of `CLA.md`, sign it, and email it to steffan.haskins@gmail.com. Then mention in your pull request that you've signed.

If you contribute on behalf of an employer, or your employer may own what you write, make sure you have permission first (see section 4 of the CLA).

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue first for anything bigger than a small fix, so we can agree on the approach.
2. Keep changes focused, and add tests (`npm test` must pass).
3. Keep the license header (SPDX line, copyright and NOTICE pointer) at the top of every source file, and add it to any new file.
4. Don't remove or change the notices required by `NOTICE` (AGPLv3 section 7(b)).
5. Never commit real journal files (`*.db`) or personal data.

## Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith.
